# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designers & User Researchers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and User Researchers focus on customer needs, usability, and product experience. They ensure that solutions are intuitive, accessible, and aligned with real user workflows.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user interviews, usability testing, and research synthesis
- Define user flows, wireframes, and interaction patterns
- Partner with Product Managers on problem framing and prioritization
- Collaborate with Developers and QA to validate feasibility, accessibility, and usability
- Translate user feedback into design improvements and acceptance criteria

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and product usability
- Reduce friction in key workflows and journeys
- Ensure design decisions are evidence-based and testable

### Typical Communication
- Discovery workshops and user research reviews
- Design critiques, stakeholder demos, and usability findings
- Collaboration with Product, Engineering, and QA on design feasibility and validation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Product Managers to refine customer problems and desired outcomes
- Partners with Developers to ensure designs are implementable and maintainable
- Coordinates with QA to validate that experience requirements are tested and accepted
- Keeps Project Managers informed of design milestones, dependencies, and stakeholder decisions

---

## Technical Leads & Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical strategy, design quality, and engineering standards. They help teams make sound trade-offs while maintaining delivery momentum.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical direction, architecture patterns, and integration standards
- Review design proposals and advise on implementation trade-offs
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and scalability constraints
- Support Developers in resolving complex engineering problems
- Collaborate with Product and Project Managers on delivery feasibility and sequencing

### Goals
- Deliver maintainable, scalable, and secure solutions
- Reduce technical debt and architecture drift
- Improve delivery predictability without compromising quality

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical design discussions
- Engineering planning sessions and dependency tracking
- Escalation support for high-risk or cross-team technical decisions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers on implementation guidance and code quality standards
- Advises Project Managers on delivery estimates, technical dependencies, and risk areas
- Collaborates with Product Managers on trade-offs related to scope, timeline, and technical feasibility
- Shares risk information with QA and stakeholders to support release readiness and testing strategy

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate stakeholder needs, business context, and process requirements into clear, actionable requirements for delivery teams.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document stakeholder needs, goals, and constraints
- Define business workflows, user journeys, and process improvements
- Refine acceptance criteria and clarify ambiguous requirements
- Support prioritization discussions and impact analysis
- Coordinate handoffs between stakeholders, Product Managers, and technical teams

### Goals
- Reduce ambiguity and improve requirement quality
- Align business needs with delivery execution
- Improve decision quality during planning and delivery

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder interviews and requirements workshops
- Product backlog refinement, definition of done discussions, and issue triage
- Cross-functional alignment meetings with Product, Project, and Engineering teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to shape the problem definition and success criteria
- Helps Project Managers understand timelines, dependencies, and operational impacts
- Collaborates with Developers and QA to ensure requirements are specific, testable, and aligned to business value
- Supports Stakeholders by translating business concerns into actionable delivery language

---

## Release / Operations Leads

### Role Summary
Release and Operations Leads ensure that software can be deployed, monitored, and supported effectively in production environments.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release planning, deployment readiness, and rollback preparation
- Own operational readiness checks and production monitoring expectations
- Help define incident response procedures, observability, and support handoff
- Partner with Engineering and QA on smoke tests and deployment verification
- Communicate release status, risks, and operational dependencies to stakeholders

### Goals
- Minimize release risk and service disruption
- Improve operational readiness and supportability
- Strengthen confidence in production deployments

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and deployment checklists
- Post-release validation and incident coordination
- Updates with Project Managers, Product Managers, and support teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Coordinates with Developers and QA to validate deployment readiness and post-deploy checks
- Works with Project Managers to align release timing with milestones and stakeholder communication
- Supports Product Managers in communicating customer-impacting changes or known issues
- Escalates operational or reliability concerns to the appropriate stakeholder group when risks increase

---

## Security & Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads help ensure that projects meet required controls, compliance expectations, and risk standards from design through delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Review project risks related to security, privacy, and compliance
- Define required controls, review gates, and validation steps
- Partner with technical teams to assess vulnerabilities and mitigations
- Support QA in verification of security and privacy requirements
- Escalate high-risk findings and coordinate response plans with stakeholders

### Goals
- Reduce exposure to security and privacy risk
- Embed controls earlier in the delivery lifecycle
- Improve trust, governance, and regulatory confidence

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews, threat assessments, and risk discussions
- Compliance checkpoints and incident briefings
- Cross-functional coordination with Product, Engineering, Project, and QA teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advises Product Managers on risk trade-offs and prioritization constraints
- Works with Technical Leads and Developers to validate mitigations and secure design decisions
- Collaborates with Project Managers on escalation paths, approvals, and timeline impacts
- Supports QA in verifying that controls and privacy requirements are tested in a meaningful way

---

## Change Management & Customer Support Representatives

### Role Summary
Change Management and Customer Support roles prepare the organization for adoption, communicate impact, and support users before and after release.

### Responsibilities
- Prepare user communications, launch messaging, and training content
- Coordinate readiness for support teams and operational handoff
- Capture customer feedback and service issues after changes are released
- Support stakeholder alignment on rollout impacts and adoption strategies
- Help identify post-launch improvement opportunities based on support patterns

### Goals
- Improve adoption and minimize user confusion after release
- Reduce customer friction during change events
- Strengthen the feedback loop between delivery and support teams

### Typical Communication
- Release announcements, stakeholder updates, and support briefings
- Customer support escalation reviews and feedback loops
- Coordination with Product, Project, and Release teams before and after launch

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to understand customer impact and adoption goals
- Helps Project Managers coordinate communications and launch readiness plans
- Coordinates with Release/Operations Leads on deployment timing and post-release monitoring
- Feeds customer issues back to Product and Engineering to inform ongoing improvements

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Smaller teams may combine multiple roles, but the responsibilities should remain clear to avoid gaps in accountability.
